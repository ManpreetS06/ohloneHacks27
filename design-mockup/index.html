// About the event

Two days to build something rad.

OhloneHacks is a free, beginner-friendly hackathon hosted by the Associated Students of Ohlone College (ASOC). Bring an idea or none at all. Form a team, go to hands-on workshops, get help from mentors, and demo what you built to industry judges.

First hackathon? Good, that's who we built it for.

// Questions?

FAQ

What is a hackathon?

An event where you turn ideas into real projects. OhloneHacks is two days of learning, building, and demoing with other people.

Is it overnight?

No. It runs over two days with no overnight hacking. Doors close at 9 PM on Day 1, so go home, sleep, and come back at 8 AM Saturday.

Who can come?

Anyone 18 or older who wants to build something is welcome to sign up.

What does it cost?

It's free. Meals on both days, swag, and workshops are all included.

I have little to no experience. Can I come?

Yes. We run beginner workshops on both days and have mentors on hand the whole time. If you leave having learned something new, that counts.

Do I have to attend the workshops?

No, they're optional. Drop in to any session that helps your project, then head back to your team.

What if I don't have a team?

You can hack solo, or meet teammates at the team mixer or on our Discord, which opens a week before the event.

How do I apply?

Fill out the registration form below. Spots are limited.
